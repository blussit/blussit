## Notes For Reviewer

**Assumptions made from how the platform works today**
- KYC today = photo, Aadhaar number + card photo, PAN number + card photo, local and permanent address. No driving licence, no live selfie, no police verification. Manager or admin verifies; editing locks once verified; a manager must reject with a note to reopen.
- Documents are in a private R2 bucket behind authorised download; managers see masked numbers.
- GPS: step-level location checks are enforced (steps refused outside the allowed area or on suspicious trails; manager/admin approval can override one step, logged), breadcrumb trail kept 30 days.
- Wallet: captain earning credited when paid online/plan; company share debited when captain holds cash; minimum balance 50 rupees; payout requests approved by manager/admin.
- Captain KYC is not required before assignment today (founder decision pending, ARCHITECTURE.md section 15).
- Masked calls are a planned feature (Contact Card, Exotel), not built. No call recording exists. Clauses are written to be safe either way.
- Tips are recorded as cash or online; the tip-sharing rule is not defined in code.
- Cancellation charges are billed to the customer wallet; captains are not charged for them.

- Section 3 (employee vs independent partner) and everything depending on it.
- Section 9.7 and 9.8 (post-exit non-solicitation; Section 27, Indian Contract Act).

- Section 8 and 16 (continuous location tracking, proportionality, consent validity).
