## Notes For Reviewer

**Assumptions made from how the platform works today**
- Managers are center-scoped; a customer is "known" to a center through a booking, plan or society enrolment there. Out-of-scope customers return not-found.
- Manager powers in code: assign/self-assign, quick booking, log a done job, mark done, edit and cancel bookings, cancellation charge reduce/waive, paybacks (booking-linked only, never free wallet credit), plan sales by link/auto-pay/cash, custom plans (discount up to 50 percent in code), pass extension (up to 10 days per period), captain KYC review, captain password reset, complaints, inventory, attendance, center KPIs.
- Admin-only: prices, holidays, recycle bin, free wallet adjustment, finance ledger, WhatsApp CRM templates and all-center views. The WhatsApp CRM inbox is admin-side today; managers receive new-booking WhatsApp alerts only. The clause on the WhatsApp inbox is written to stay valid if managers are given access.
- Audit logs capture every staff change with before and after and are kept forever.
- KYC numbers are masked for managers; documents are private; editing locks once verified.
- No call recording or masked calls exist today (Contact Card is planned, not built).
- Manager WhatsApp for staff accounts is limited; staff phone is not proof of identity until self-verified.

**Clauses that most need a lawyer**
- Section 3 (employee vs partner vs franchise-style) and the consequences.
- Sections 9.14 and 18.2 (deductions, liability; Code on Wages).

- Section 10 (monitoring and consent for employee surveillance).
- Section 6 and 13 (DPDP: manager as processor vs employee, purpose limitation).
- Section 20 (arbitration vs labour forum).
